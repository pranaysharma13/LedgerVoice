| D35 | Audit evolution | RESOLVED state (from NEEDS_ATTENTION, note required); schema versioned with PRAGMA user_version + ordered migrations; transfer ids must match `LV-TRF-<8 hex>` | Incidents can be closed; M1d can add columns safely; reconcile can always see legs | Accepted 2026-11-19 (review R1-05/R1-09/R1-14) |
| D36 | Referential integrity | Nessie has none (verified live 2026-11-20): deleting an account keeps its transactions; transactions can be created for nonexistent accounts. Backends (incl. the fake) store what they're given; existence is checked explicitly with get_account by the saga (before any write) and by reconcile (account_missing, while still checking orphaned legs) | The fake must copy Nessie's quirks (D20); a deposit to a mistyped id would otherwise vanish | Accepted 2026-11-20 |
| D37 | Concurrency model | Per-account locks are process-wide (shared by every TransferService); the audit log is thread-safe; one process per audit DB (multi-process would need a DB-level lock). Reconcile flags `overdrawn` on non-credit accounts as a safety net | Two service instances could otherwise both pass the funds check and overspend | Accepted 2026-11-20 (review V1-01) |
| D38 | Resolution snapshot | `resolve()` stores the leg ids the human saw; reconcile flags any later leg as `leg_after_resolution`, and flags a tagged leg from a transfer outside the scanned scope as `out_of_scope_leg` | "Resolved" means "checked at that moment", not "ignore forever"; a late write must stay visible | Accepted 2026-11-20 (review V1-02/V1-07) |
| D39 | Rejections at execution | Only the customer who created a transfer can execute/cancel it. A temporary failure while re-checking (network, 5xx) raises `TransferRejected(retryable=True)` and keeps it PENDING; a permanent one (account gone, insufficient funds) cancels it. The destination is re-checked just before leg 2; if gone, leg 1 is compensated. Migration steps are atomic (`BEGIN IMMEDIATE`) | A blip right after "yes" must not discard the request; a stranger's session must not touch it; no deposit into a deleted account | Accepted 2026-11-20 (review V1-03/V1-04/V1-05/V1-06) |
| D40 | Mid-transfer account checks fail closed | Account existence checks during a transfer return exists / gone / unknown, and each caller decides what "unknown" means: before leg 2, gone or unknown → leg 2 not written → leg 1 returned; before the reversal, gone or unknown → NEEDS_ATTENTION, no reversal written. Only temporary errors make a rejection retryable (exact rule refined by D41); 400/401/403 cancel. `ReconcileReport.count()` rejects unknown kinds | A failed check is not proof an account exists; never tell the user money was "returned" into a deleted account; a retry loop on a 401 never ends; a typo in a safety check must not silently count zero | Accepted 2026-11-20 (V2 N1/N2/N3) |
| D41 | Ambiguous ≠ temporary | Two separate rules in `errors.py`: `is_ambiguous` ("might a write have happened?", used by the saga, unchanged) and `is_temporary` ("will retrying help?", used for `retryable`): transport failure with no status, 5xx, 429. Bad data from the server is raised as `NessieDataError` (a `NessieError` subclass) and is never temporary, but stays ambiguous for creates | A malformed record has no HTTP status, like a timeout, but retrying returns the same data forever; a garbled create response must still mean "go and look" (D33) | Accepted 2026-11-20 (V2 finding 5) |
| D42 | Seed write failures | Any error while seeding aborts the run; recovery = reset + reseed into fresh accounts (no resume) | Always ends in a known clean state; a live seed takes only minutes; resume logic adds half-seeded states | Accepted 2026-11-23 |
| D43 | Malformed records on our own accounts | Stay strict: list reads on our accounts raise NessieDataError (only shared merchants are skipped); seed read-back verification guarantees seeded data is well-formed | A skipped record makes a balance silently wrong; "can't read that balance" is better than a wrong number | Accepted 2026-11-23 |
| D44 | mypy scope | Strict for `packages/nessie-tools/src` and `src/ledgervoice` ([tool.mypy]); tests type-checked by a second run in `make lint` with relaxed flags (`MYPY_TESTS_FLAGS`, keeps `check_untyped_defs`). `src/ledgervoice` ships `py.typed` | Catches calls to removed/renamed APIs in tests (the v2 `.execute()` bug) without annotating every fixture; mypy override globs can't match top-level test modules | Accepted 2026-11-23 (R1-18) |
| D45 | Credit card balance | Report a credit card as amount owed = completed purchases − payments (a positive number), not a negative balance | Matches how people talk ("I owe $340"), so answers and ground truth use the user's framing | Accepted 2026-11-23 |
| D46 | M1c delivery | Two parts: M1c-1 (diagnostic, generator, ground truth; offline) and M1c-2 (applier, manifest, read-back verification, reset, live); review cycle 2 covers M1c + M1d (D30) | Smaller, reviewable deliveries; the generator is validated before anything writes to Nessie | Accepted 2026-11-23 |
| D47 | Nessie creates validate almost nothing | Verified live 2026-11-23: accounts are accepted under unknown customer ids and purchases under unknown merchant ids; bill `status` is validated (`BillStatus`: pending/recurring/cancelled/completed). The fake copies all three; both backends reject a bad bill status before sending. Our code checks explicitly: the seeder only uses ids it created, read-back verifies every purchase's merchant, and spending tools must show unknown-merchant purchases as category "unknown", never drop them | A typo'd merchant id would silently produce an uncategorized purchase and wrong category totals; the fake must behave like Nessie (D20) | Accepted 2026-11-23 |
| D48 | Seed money-flow rules | Opening balance = stored Nessie balance at the window start. Income = completed deposits to checking. Paid bill = completed bill + same-day same-amount purchase at the biller; next bill after today = pending, no money moves; one cancelled bill. Subscriptions on the card. Reserve = a month of bills at their highest + the savings transfer: optional debit spending never takes checking below it. Card paid on the 20th: the statement (completed card purchases before the 1st, minus payments) in full when that keeps the reserve, else as much as it can. Savings transfer on the 25th. Internal moves (savings, card payments) are flagged `internal`, carry no LV-TRF tag, and count as neither income nor spending. Some purchases in the last 3 days are pending. No tags in descriptions; records are matched through the manifest. Spending = completed purchases unless asked otherwise; date ranges inclusive | Ground truth must equal compute_balance (D18) exactly; realistic behaviour (rent money is kept back) also made the generator robust: 0 failures in 1,000 seed/date plans, versus 13 of 240 before the reserve | Accepted 2026-11-23/24 |
| D49 | Bills always carry a recurring day | Verified live 2026-11-23 (`make test-live`): Nessie accepts a bill without `recurring_date`, but from then on every list of that account's bills fails with HTTP 400 (its own response model requires `recurring_date` and `upcoming_payment_date`). `create_bill` therefore requires `recurring_date` (1-31) on the interface and both backends, checked by `require_recurring_day` before any request; every seeded bill has one. A live contract test documents the server behaviour on a throwaway account | One bad bill would make an account's bills unreadable for good ("what bills do I have?" fails); the check costs nothing | Accepted 2026-11-23 |
| D50 | Travel category | Add `travel` to the D24 taxonomy; airline and hotel merchants move there. Persona budgets: Priya's family support is $450 (expected monthly net about +$7; it was -$143 at $300), and a test keeps every persona's expected monthly net at or above zero | "Housing" should mean rent; an overspending persona makes unrealistic data and once crashed the generator | Accepted 2026-11-23 |
